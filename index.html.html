About Us

NewsVocab was built by a developer who wanted a simpler way to grow an English vocabulary without extra study time. Most of us already read the news every day, so the idea was to turn that habit into learning.

What we do

The NewsVocab Chrome extension reads the English article you have open, selects useful words, and gives you the meaning and an example sentence for each. You can download everything as a Word document and revise whenever you like.

Our approach

Learning works best in context, so every word comes from an article you chose to read. We keep a record of your earlier words so the same ones don’t keep coming back.

Pricing

Premium access costs Rs. 9.00 for 21 days. We want it to be affordable for every learner.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what NewsVocab collects and how it is used. By using the extension or this website you agree to it.

Information we handle

  • Article text: when you press Generate Words, the text of the article in your active tab is sent to our server and on to an AI service so that vocabulary words can be selected. We do not read other tabs or browse your history.
  • Your word list: the words generated for you may be kept so earlier words are not repeated.
  • Contact details: if you email us, we receive the address and message you send.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by a third-party payment provider. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: choosing words, tracking your premium plan dates, and replying to your messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third parties

Article text is processed by an AI provider to generate the words, and payments are processed by a payment provider. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can uninstall the extension at any time, and you can ask us to delete the data we hold about you by contacting us.

Changes
