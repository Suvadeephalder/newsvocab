About Us

NewsVocab was built by a developer who wanted a simpler way to grow an English vocabulary without extra study time. Most of us already read the news every day, so the idea was to turn that habit into learning.

What we do

The NewsVocab Chrome extension reads the English article you have open, selects useful words, and gives you the meaning and an example sentence for each. You can download everything as a Word document and revise whenever you like.

Our approach

Learning works best in context, so every word comes from an article you chose to read. We keep a record of your earlier words so the same ones don’t keep coming back.

Pricing

Premium access costs Rs. 9.00 for 21 days. We want it to be affordable for every learner.

Privacy Policy

In short: We collect your email address to create your account and give you premium access. When you press Generate Words, the text of the article you have open is sent to our server and an AI service to pick vocabulary. 1. Who we are

NewsVocab (“the extension”) is a Chrome browser extension, with this companion website, developed and operated by the NewsVocab developer. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at xteam.care@gmail.com.

2. What the extension does

NewsVocab has a single purpose: it selects useful vocabulary words from the English news article you are reading and shows each word’s meaning and an example sentence, which you can download as a Word document.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the extension and your premium plan:

  • Email address (Gmail): collected when you sign up or sign in. We use it to identify your account, save your data, and check whether you have premium access.
  • Article text: only when you press Generate Words, the text of the article in your active tab is sent to our server, and on to an AI service, so vocabulary words can be selected. The extension does not read your other tabs or your browsing history, and it does nothing in the background.
  • Your word list: the words generated for you are saved against your account so the same words are not repeated.
  • Mobile number and email for payment: required by our payment partner, Razorpay, when you buy premium. You enter these on Razorpay’s checkout.
  • Payment status: we receive confirmation from Razorpay that a payment succeeded or failed, with a transaction ID and plan dates.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as your daily word limit, stored in your browser and/or linked to your account.
  • Messages to us: if you use the Contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and message.

We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details. These are entered directly with Razorpay and never reach us. We also do not collect your browsing history, passwords, or the content of pages you did not ask us to process.

We use your email address to manage your account, unlock and verify premium access, send payment receipts, and reply to support requests. Article text is used only to generate your vocabulary words. 6. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone.

We share data only with service providers that help us run NewsVocab, and only as needed:

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights and users’ safety. No human reads your article text or word list, except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with your consent.

7. Limited Use

The use of information received from Google accounts and the browser will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide and improve the NewsVocab features described above. We do not transfer it to others except as described in this policy, we do not use it for advertising or for credit or lending decisions, and we do not allow humans to read it except as noted in section 6.

8. Remote code

The extension does not load or run remotely hosted code. All code is included in the package you install from the Chrome Web Store. The extension sends article text to our server as data only, and receives words and meanings as data only.

9. Cookies and tracking

The extension does not use tracking, analytics profiling or fingerprinting technologies. The Razorpay checkout page may use its own cookies to process payments securely; these are controlled by Razorpay.

10. Data security
